The Brief

Many businesses get traffic but lose leads because visitors do not get answers fast enough. This service is built for brands that want a smarter on-site assistant to handle common questions, capture contact details, and route high-intent users to the right next step.

The Challenge

  • Visitors often leave before submitting a form or booking a call.
  • Teams spend time answering the same questions over and over.
  • There is no automated way to qualify leads outside business hours.

The Solution

I implement a chatbot flow that fits the brand voice, answers frequent questions, and guides users toward conversions. The setup can include FAQ training, lead capture prompts, booking handoff, and escalation to email or WhatsApp when a human response is needed. The goal is to reduce friction while making the site feel more responsive and helpful.
