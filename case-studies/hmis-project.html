HMIS Project

A healthcare management system designed to centralize patient records, appointment scheduling, billing, and reporting into one clearer workflow.

The Brief

The HMIS project needed a cleaner digital workspace for staff to manage patient information, schedules, and daily admin tasks without jumping between disconnected screens. The goal was to make the system easier to use on both desktop and mobile while keeping the interface calm and practical for healthcare workflows.

The Challenge

  • Important data was spread across multiple screens and manual processes.
  • Staff needed faster access to records, appointments, and reports.
  • The interface had to stay readable and efficient for busy admin users.

The Solution

I structured the HMIS around clear dashboard sections, role-based panels, and streamlined navigation. The design focuses on quick access to patient records, appointment scheduling, billing views, and reporting tools, with an interface hierarchy that reduces friction and helps staff move faster through routine tasks.
