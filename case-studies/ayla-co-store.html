Ayla & Co Store

A clean, conversion-focused Shopify storefront showcasing product photography, simplified collections, and an optimized checkout for boutique retail.

The Brief

Ayla & Co needed a stylish online store that reflected their brand and made shopping effortless on mobile. The goal was to increase average order value and decrease checkout friction while keeping the design elegant and image-forward.

The Challenge

  • Unclear product organization and inconsistent image sizes.
  • High checkout abandonment due to poor mobile UX.
  • Lack of promotional flows and collection management.

The Solution

I designed custom collection templates, implemented responsive image handling, and improved the checkout UX with clearer CTAs and trust signals. I added promo sections and simplified navigation to reduce friction and highlight seasonal collections.
