IT Inventory Store

The Brief

The IT inventory store needed a cleaner way to track devices, stock, and asset movement without losing visibility over items in circulation.

The Challenge

  • Asset records were scattered across spreadsheets.
  • Issue and return tracking needed to be simple.
  • The team needed a quick view of what was in stock.

The Solution

I structured the system around inventory lists, asset details, and stock management so the team could understand availability and movement at a glance.
