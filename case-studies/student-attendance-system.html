Student Attendance System

The Brief

The student attendance system needed to make class tracking easier for staff and reduce the amount of manual attendance recording.

The Challenge

  • Class attendance was being updated by hand.
  • Section-wise tracking had to stay simple and fast.
  • Reports needed to be easy to scan for missing students.

The Solution

I structured the workflow around sections, attendance entry, and reporting so the team could mark attendance quickly and review trends without extra steps.
