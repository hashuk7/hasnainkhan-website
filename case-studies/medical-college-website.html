Medical College Website

Modernizing an institutional website to streamline course information, admissions flow, and improve accessibility for prospective students and faculty.

The Brief

The medical college required a modern website to present courses, faculty profiles, and an admissions guide clearly. The old site was hard to navigate and wasn't accessible on mobile devices, creating friction for prospective students.

The Challenge

  • Outdated information architecture that made it hard to find course details.
  • Poor mobile and accessibility support for prospective students.
  • Complex content managed inconsistently across pages.

The Solution

I built a WordPress custom theme with a clear content model for courses, faculty, and events. Improvements included searchable course pages, structured faculty profiles, accessible markup (ARIA roles and better semantics), and an events calendar. The CMS was organized so staff can update admissions timelines and course content without developer help.
