The Brief

Suleman Roshan Medical College required a robust, modern, and purely custom web platform to present academic programs, prospective student admissions, clinical departments, and institutional credibility. Alongside the main public institutional website, I also architected and contributed to five specialized internal systems used across the institution: QEC Portal, Admission Portal, Staff Attendance System, Student Attendance System, and IT Inventory Store.

The Challenge

  • Legacy and fragmented architecture that made it difficult to find program and faculty details.
  • Poor mobile responsiveness and accessibility for prospective applicants and students.
  • Need for high security, fast database queries, and custom academic workflows without CMS bloat.
  • Multiple internal departments needed dedicated, reliable portals for quality control, admissions, attendance tracking, and hardware inventory.

The Solution

I engineered a purely custom web platform from the ground up, built specifically for the institution's scale and requirements. The custom architecture features a clean data model for courses, faculty profiles, and institutional announcements, paired with optimized semantic HTML, modern CSS, and responsive JavaScript. In parallel, I contributed to the QEC Portal, Admission Portal, Staff Attendance System, Student Attendance System, and IT Inventory Store, giving each department a purpose-built, high-speed interface without the overhead or security risks of third-party CMS plugins.
